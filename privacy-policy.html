EV Pocket — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Summary

EV Pocket does not collect your data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not use the network.

What the app does with the camera

EV Pocket uses the camera to measure light. Camera frames are analyzed in memory on your device. They are not uploaded or shared, and they are not saved — except a single viewfinder snapshot attached to a notebook entry when you tap “Log shot” (you can turn this off in Settings → Viewfinder). The app never saves photos to your photo library and never reads it. Camera is the only permission the app requests, and it is used only so the meter can work. You can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location or diagnostics. The app has no backend and does not contact any server, because we do not run any.

Data stored on your device

  • Shot notebook: notes you save (film, aperture, shutter, exposure values, comments) and, if enabled, the viewfinder snapshot taken when you log a shot, are stored locally on your device.
  • Settings and calibration: your preferences, camera/lens limits and calibration offset are stored locally.
  • Optional reference photo: if you choose to attach a photo to a notebook entry, you pick it yourself using the system photo picker. The app receives only the photo you pick and does not get access to your photo library.

This data stays on your device. If you use iCloud or computer backups of your device, iOS may include app data in those backups under your own backup settings. We do not have access to them.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro unlock is handled by Apple through StoreKit. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether the purchase is valid. We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID information. There are no subscriptions.

Third parties

EV Pocket does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Delete notebook entries inside the app, or delete the app to remove its local data.
  • Turn off the viewfinder snapshot in Settings → Viewfinder.
  • Revoke camera access in iOS Settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above changed.
